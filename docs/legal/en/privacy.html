3. External AI: information, choice and control

  1. Recipients and purpose: Your selected Anthropic Claude Code, OpenAI Codex CLI or Google Gemini CLI and its provider process text recognition or AI summaries. CLIPARY runs a CLI installed on your PC, rather than routing requests through an AI proxy operated by CLIPARY's operator.
  2. Information and timing: When an external AI request runs, the selected image file and app-generated analysis instructions are given to the CLI and transmitted to the provider's service. Images may contain visible personal information, documents or conversations. The CLI may also process account, authentication, IP address, device and usage information under its own policies.
  3. Your confirmation: Every AI request identifies the selected provider and image/instruction transmission and asks for confirmation. Cancelling prevents that request from being sent. New users default to built-in Tesseract OCR. Existing saved AI OCR preferences may remain, but external-transmission confirmation still applies.
  4. Installation, sign-in and connection checks: If you choose to connect AI, after installation confirmation the app may install the external CLI through Anthropic's official installer or npm and open the provider's sign-in screen. The external CLI manages sign-in and authentication details. Gemini sign-in verification sends a short test request without a screen image. Prestarting the selected Gemini CLI or checking its connection may also cause it to contact its service.
  5. Retention and international processing: Storage locations, retention periods, model-improvement use and international-processing conditions for AI requests/responses, authentication and CLI session records vary by provider, account and settings. Deleting CLIPARY's temporary files does not delete CLI or provider records. Check current provider policies and account settings: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google.
  6. Refusal and withdrawal: Cancel transmission confirmation, or select Built-in OCR only (Tesseract) in Settings → AI → Text recognition and do not run AI summaries, to avoid sending images to AI. Sign-out, CLI removal and deletion of previously sent records also require the CLI/provider's procedures. Refusing consent does not restrict ordinary capture, editing, recording or built-in OCR.

Before using external AI, check whether the selected image contains another person's personal information, health/financial information, passwords or confidential material, and obtain necessary authority and consent. A general transmission confirmation does not by itself satisfy all consent from others or separate consent required by law.
